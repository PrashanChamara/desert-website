Tor & Privacy Search — Full Explanation What it is, who uses it, and how to win there

What Is Tor Search?

Tor (The Onion Router) is a privacy network used by people who want to browse anonymously — journalists, privacy-conscious users, expats avoiding ISP tracking, and VPN users in the UAE. When using Tor Browser, users access normal search engines (Google, DuckDuckGo, Startpage) through anonymized routing. There is no separate "Tor index" — the results shown are the same Google or DuckDuckGo results, just accessed privately.

Who Uses Tor in the UAE Context?

In the UAE, many expat families use VPNs or Tor to bypass ISP restrictions or for privacy reasons. A parent searching "cricket academy Dubai" on DuckDuckGo via Tor will see the same results as a standard DuckDuckGo search. Winning on DuckDuckGo = winning on Tor. The only extra consideration: your site must work properly with JavaScript disabled (Tor's highest security setting). Your Flask server-rendered pages are perfect for this — they don't require JS to display content.

Action: Ensure Site Works Without JavaScript

Test your site with JavaScript disabled in your browser. All core content (location info, phone numbers, enrolment CTA) should be visible without JS. The sponsor carousel and IPL prediction widget will break — that's fine. The content that matters for parents must load without JS. This also benefits screen readers and accessibility scoring.
