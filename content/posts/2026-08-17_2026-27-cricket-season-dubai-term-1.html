A competitive player-development pathway

As an ECB affiliated cricket academy, Desert Cubs players have opportunities to participate in up to 10 competitive tournaments per season. This structure helps players move from training into real match situations, with examples including the DC Premier League, DC Emerging League and the ECB National Junior Tournament.

This tournament exposure is part of the wider Desert Cubs player-development pathway, supported by regular coaching, match awareness, performance habits and competition experience. Parents can view the academy tournament structure on the Desert Cubs tournaments page.
