What to Expect This Summer

Every session at our Summer Camp 2026 is structured with purpose. Our ICC-certified coaching staff will work with your child across three core pillars that define champions at every level:

Fast Bowling Masterclass

Stop worrying about the run-up and start focusing on your mechanics. Our coaches will break down your action, focusing on posture, arm speed, hip rotation, and follow-through to add those crucial extra yards of pace. Whether you're a natural outswing bowler or a raw pace merchant, this masterclass will give you the technical foundation to dominate.

Advanced Batting Techniques

Learn to dominate any bowling attack. We focus on footwork, head position, and shot selection against both pace and spin, ensuring you're ready for the hard, bouncy tracks and the slow turners alike. From the pull shot and the cover drive, to the sweep and the reverse sweep — every weapon in your armoury will be sharpened.

Mental Toughness & Strategy

Cricket is 90% mental. This camp includes dedicated sessions on match awareness, situational pressure management, and building the "big-game" temperament required for international tours and high-pressure finals. Champions don't just have talent — they have the mental framework to deploy it when it matters most.
