Desert Cubs Global Cricket Pathway

Winston Weeks

Global Consultant| Desert Cubs Cricket Academy UAE

Local talent.
Global opportunities.

Winston Weeks brings extensive international cricket experience, having played in England, Zimbabwe and his native Barbados.

Throughout his cricketing journey, he has supported emerging young cricketers in identifying opportunities to develop their careers in England and other parts of the world.

As Global Consultant to Desert Cubs Cricket Academy UAE, Winston is helping strengthen connections with county cricket organisations, international clubs and the wider cricketing community.

His role focuses on identifying promising Desert Cubs players, developing global pathways and connecting emerging talent with appropriate opportunities for international exposure.

For cricket families

A few things
you may be wondering.

What is Desert Cubs Cricket Academy?

Founded in Sharjah in 2007, Desert Cubs has trained more than 15,000 alumni and runs five active centres across Dubai and Sharjah. Read the academy story.

Where can my child train?

Centres are at Sharjah English School, Delhi Private School, Deira International School, Star International School and Baseline Sports Academy. Explore the centres or compare sessions in the weekly timetable.

What experience levels can join?

Desert Cubs has junior pathways from foundation cricket through competitive age-group squads. Session groups vary by centre and day; see the timetable or contact a centre to discuss a suitable session.

Is girls’ cricket coaching available?

Yes. Girls are welcome across the academy. Desert Cubs’ girls’ cricket history includes UAE Women’s Cricket Captain Esha Oza and the first ECB Women’s League title in 2017. Explore the girls’ programme.

Where can players train in Sharjah?

Sharjah English School on Maliha Road has a natural grass ground, six centre turf pitches, floodlights, a viewing pavilion and video-analysis facilities. See SES centre details and training times.

Are indoor cricket and holiday camps available?

Baseline Sports Academy in Dubai Investment Park is an air-conditioned indoor centre with five cricket lanes. Camp dates vary by season; contact the academy for upcoming availability. See published camp details.

What tournaments and playing opportunities are available?

Players can take part in academy and ECB competitions, and Desert Cubs organises junior international tours. Explore tournaments and tour information and completed journeys.

How do I find current fees and registration information?

Programme fees and availability vary by centre and session. Check the official registration portal or contact Desert Cubs on WhatsApp for current details.
