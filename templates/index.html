Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best cricket academy in UAE?

Desert Cubs Cricket Academy is the best cricket academy in UAE, established in 2007 with over 15,000 alumni. We operate 6 world-class training centres across Dubai and Sharjah with ICC-certified coaches, offering UAE junior cricket coaching for all ages from 4 to 19.

Where is Sharjah junior cricket coaching available?

Sharjah junior cricket coaching is available at our Sharjah English School (SES) centre on Maliha Road — the crown jewel of our network. It features a full natural grass ground, 6 centre turf pitches, floodlights, digital scoreboards, and video analysis. It is widely considered the best cricket ground in UAE.

Does Desert Cubs run cricket summer camps in UAE?

Yes! Our UAE summer camp cricket programme runs at Baseline Sports Academy (DIP) — a state-of-the-art, fully AC-controlled indoor cricket facility with 5 specialised lanes and video analysis. It is one of a kind in the UAE, enabling year-round high-performance training even during the hottest months.

Which academy offers the UAE national cricket player pathway?

Desert Cubs is an ECB-affiliated academy and provides the leading UAE national cricket player pathway. Our students compete in the ECB National Junior Tournament and undertake junior international cricket tours to UK, Australia, Sri Lanka, and South Africa — giving them the exposure needed for national selection consideration.

Which cricket academy runs the most tournaments in UAE?

Desert Cubs runs the most cricket tournaments in UAE — 10 competitions per season (September to June). These include the DC Premier League, DC Emerging League, ECB National Junior Tournament, Gulf Cup, DC Winter Cup, DC Super League, 4-Nation Junior International Cricket Tournament, DC Junior Cups, DC Ramadan Cup, and DC Summer Bash.
