About The Development

A Future Address In South Asia

Home Lands Port City Colombo is planned for Land Lot 77 at the Central Park District of Port City Colombo. The development brings a twin-tower luxury residential concept to one of Sri Lanka's most ambitious urban destinations, with ocean, park and financial district views.

  • 3 acres total land with 2 acres development area and 1 acre dedicated park.
  • Developed together with Port City Authority, with the project elevated 4 feet above ground level.
  • Architecture and development planning by SOG + Philip Weerarathna.
  • CS2 grading development company with a landmark G+35 tower structure.
