Common Questions

Summer Camp FAQ

Who can join the summer camp?

The 2026 Summer Camp is open for junior cricketers aged 8 to 16. Players will be grouped by age and ability where needed.

Which locations are available?

You can register for DPS Gardens, Baseline Sports Academy in DIP, or SIS Al Qusais. Each location has its own weekly training days and session time.

Can my child attend a single session?

Yes. Single sessions are available at AED 150. Package options are also available for 12, 18 and 24 sessions.

How do I register?

Use the online registration form on this page. You can also call or WhatsApp +971 58 827 4266 for help with location, package and availability.
