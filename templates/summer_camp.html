Common Questions

Summer Camp FAQ

Does my child need to know cricket before joining?

Absolutely not. Our Mini Cubs and Junior Cubs groups are perfect for complete beginners. Coaches start from the very basics and no experience is required to join any age group.

What equipment does my child need to bring?

All cricket equipment is provided at the Baseline Sports Academy facility. Children should wear comfortable sports clothing and bring a water bottle. Cricket whites are not required.

Can girls join the summer camp?

Yes — absolutely. Desert Cubs runs UAE's leading girls cricket programme year-round. Girls are fully welcome at our summer camp alongside our dedicated girls-only sessions throughout the year.

How do I book a place?

The easiest way is to WhatsApp us directly. Our team will confirm availability, share the exact schedule and pricing, and get your child registered quickly.

Are the coaches qualified?

Yes. All Desert Cubs coaches hold ICC or ECB coaching certifications and have undergone safeguarding training. Our founder Presley Polonnowita holds ECB Level 2 certification and has developed cricket in the UAE since 2007.
