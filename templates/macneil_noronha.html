Macneil's Own Desert Cubs Words

Before the IPL headlines, Macneil documented what Desert Cubs meant to him as a junior player. In the 2015 Desert Cubs Tour of Australia souvenir, he wrote about joining the academy in September 2014 and training four days a week.

The article mentions technical training, regular match opportunities, one-on-one coaching, wicketkeeping work, fitness guidance, and support from coaches and family. That archive now sits beside his CSK selection as evidence of a long player-development journey.

This is the kind of pathway Desert Cubs aims to build: a young player starts with structured fundamentals, competes regularly, receives detailed coaching, and keeps progressing until larger opportunities arrive.
