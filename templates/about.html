The Desert Cubs Story

Legends of The Future

Desert Cubs Cricket Academy is the largest, best-equipped registered cricket academy in the Gulf region. Born from a passion by founder Presley Polonnowita to nurture young talent into great sportsmen and sportswomen, we have grown from a modest group of 11 youngsters at a corner net of Sharjah International Cricket Stadium to the UAE's premier cricket institution.

We are a registered academy in UAE, affiliated with the Emirates Cricket Board, Dubai Sports Council, Dubai Cricket Council, Sharjah Cricket Council, and Ajman Cricket Council. Our coaches hold ICC certifications, and our programme is designed with a well-structured curriculum using the latest equipment and technology.

Our 2024/2025 revenue of USD 1.63 million reflects a business model that is scalable, profitable, and built on steady year-on-year growth — proof that when you invest in young talent, everything else follows.

What Drives Us

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

"To be the best Cricket Academy in UAE admired for its effectiveness, methods, quality and performance."

Our long-term goal is to establish a Sporting Centre of Excellence with training and coaching facilities for all major sports in the Gulf, producing 'LEGENDS OF THE FUTURE' who can adapt to the current and future challenges of the domestic and international sports arena.

Our Mission

"To produce quality sportsmen and sportswomen who can respond well to the current and future challenges of Domestic and International Cricket."

We focus on embedding basic skills in young athletes at the starting point of their careers — coaching curricula designed to gradually instil all the necessary skills so they are fully equipped for challenges of different forms of the game.
