Academy Leagues

Internal academy leagues are automatically available to all enrolled students. No separate registration needed. These include the DC Premier League, Emerging League, Winter Cup, Super League, Ramadan Cup, Junior Cups, and Summer Bash.

National & Regional

External competitions like the ECB National Junior Tournament, Gulf Cup, and the 4 Nation Tournament are merit-based selections. Coaches select players based on performance and development.
