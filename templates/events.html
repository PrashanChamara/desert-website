Desert Cubs Cricket Academy has hosted multiple High Performance Programs conducted by former international cricketers and world-class coaches. These exclusive masterclasses give our junior players in the UAE a rare opportunity to train alongside some of the greatest names in the sport.

Robin Uthappa

Indian International Cricketer • Academy Visit

Former Indian cricketer Robin Uthappa visited Desert Cubs Cricket Academy on 23rd and 24th May 2026, meeting our young players across Desert Cubs centres.

He shared thoughts from his cricket journey, answered questions from players, spent time with players and families, took photos, and passed on important lessons for the next generation.

In The Spotlight

Highlight Programs

🇿🇦 November 2019 • SES & DPS Centres

Jonty Rhodes

South African Fielding Legend • 3-Day Masterclass

One of cricket's greatest ever fielders, Jonty Rhodes delivered an unforgettable 3-day masterclass at Desert Cubs' SES and DPS centres in November 2019. Hundreds of young cricketers had the privilege of learning fielding techniques, athletic movement, and mental toughness directly from the legend himself.

The program covered ground fielding, catching drills, relay throws, and the mental discipline that made Jonty one of the world's best all-round cricketers.

🇱🇰 September 2022 • Academy Programme

Marvan Atapattu

Former Sri Lanka Captain & Head Coach

Former Sri Lanka captain and head coach Marvan Atapattu led an intensive technical batting masterclass for Desert Cubs players. Known for his technically correct batting and leadership of the Sri Lanka national team, Atapattu focused on batting fundamentals, footwork, and building match temperament in young cricketers.
