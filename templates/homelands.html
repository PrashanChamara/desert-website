What is Greendale Retirement Resort and who is it for?
Greendale Retirement Resort in Athurugiriya is Sri Lanka's first international-standard retirement village — designed exclusively for senior citizens aged 50 years and above. Set across 20 acres, it offers 250 fully furnished Californian-style luxury cottages and 96 apartment suites (1BR from AED 134,000; 2BR from AED 161,500). Designed by renowned architect Philip Weeraratne. All 50+ amenities include 24/7 care services, medical centre, butler service, minimart, pharmacy, gym, and age-friendly recreational activities. What makes BayFonte Marina different from other Sri Lanka property projects?
BayFonte Marina Resort in Negombo is Sri Lanka's first tourist resort-style residential complex on a lagoon — a concept completely unprecedented in Sri Lanka. It offers private yacht parking, jet ski club, seaplane facilities, and direct Negombo Lagoon access, combined with elegant lagoon-facing residences. It won Best Waterfront Condo Development at PropertyGuru Asia Property Awards 2025. Located just 7 minutes from Bandaranaike International Airport, it is ideal for both a second home and a high-yield holiday rental investment.
