Beginner Plastic Ball Squad

A Confident First Step Into Cricket

The Desert Cubs Beginner Plastic Ball Squad at SES is designed for children who are starting cricket or still building their basic skills. Sessions introduce batting, bowling, catching, movement, teamwork and session discipline through age-appropriate drills. Coaches monitor each child and recommend progression when the player is ready for the next squad.

Plastic-ball coaching provides a safer entry format for new players while they learn the movements, awareness and habits that support future hard-ball progression.

  • A welcoming first session focused on confidence, listening and simple skill checks.
  • Direct coaching for batting, bowling, catching, movement and fielding fundamentals.
  • Progression based on skill development, confidence, attendance and readiness—not an invented age cutoff.

Frequently asked questions

Sharjah Cricket Coaching and Facility FAQs

Where is the Desert Cubs Sharjah branch?

The Sharjah branch trains at Sharjah English School on Maliha Road. Use the verified directions link on this page and contact the SES team for current entry and parking guidance.

Can a complete beginner join?

Yes. The Beginner Plastic Ball Squad introduces new players to cricket fundamentals through age-appropriate coaching. Availability and squad placement are confirmed by the SES team.

What should a child expect during the first session?

Coaches welcome the player, introduce simple batting, bowling, catching and movement activities, and observe confidence and current skill level before recommending the suitable next step.

How much does cricket coaching start from?

Sharjah coaching plans start from AED 360 per month. Conditions apply. The SES team will confirm the suitable squad, training frequency and current availability.

Which squads train at SES?

The SES pathway includes Beginners, Girls, Emerging and age-group squads through U19, together with senior and performance practice.

Does SES host cricket matches and tournaments?

Yes. SES supports centre-wicket sessions, practice matches and tournament activity, including frequent matches during active competition periods.

Can teams hire the facility?

Yes. Approved bookings may include team training, nets, practice matches, tournaments, corporate cricket and other cricket-related events. Availability and conditions apply.

What facilities are available?

SES has a natural grass ground, six centre turf pitches, seven practice nets, floodlights, a digital scoreboard, a video analysis room and a gymnasium.

How does a player move to the next squad?

Coaches assess skill development, confidence, attendance and readiness before recommending progression to the next suitable squad.
