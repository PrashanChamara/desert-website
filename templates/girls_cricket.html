Why Choose Desert Cubs

The UAE's Premier
Girls Cricket Academy

We've been pioneering girls cricket in the UAE since 2007. Our programme is built specifically for female cricketers — from the coaching methodology to the environment.

Female Head Coach

Judith Jose Peter — ICC Level 1 certified, former UAE Women's Cricket player. Our dedicated female coach leads all girls sessions with expert insight and lived experience.

Safe Environment

All female sessions are carefully structured with appropriate facilities, female coaching staff present, and safeguarding practices that give parents complete peace of mind.

ECB Champions 2017

Desert Cubs made history in 2017 by winning the inaugural ECB Women's League in the UAE — cementing our status as the region's leading girls cricket academy.

All Ages Welcome

Girls aged 4–19 are welcome regardless of experience. Whether your daughter has never held a bat or is ready for competitive cricket, we have the right programme.

ICC-Certified Coaching

Every coach holds ICC or ECB certification. Girls receive the same world-class coaching standard used to develop professional cricketers at the highest level.

National Pathway

Multiple Desert Cubs girls have progressed to UAE national squads. We maintain active links with cricket boards to give talented girls the exposure they deserve.

Programme Structure

Ages 4–8 Cubs Starter

Fun, games-based introduction to cricket. Focus on hand-eye coordination, basic movement, and falling in love with the sport.

Ages 9–13 Cubs Development

Technical foundations built correctly. Batting, bowling, and fielding drills delivered by certified coaches with match-play experience.

Ages 14–19 Cubs Elite

Advanced training for competitive cricketers. Tournament preparation, UAE national squad pathway, and individual performance coaching.

UAE & Gulf Region Cricket History

ECB Women’s League Champions
May 2017 — A Moment That Changed UAE Women’s Cricket

Desert Cubs Cricket Academy made history by winning the inaugural Emirates Cricket Board Women’s Cricket League — the first women’s cricket league ever organised in the UAE and Gulf region. This was not just a championship; it was a watershed moment for women’s cricket across the Gulf.

As Reported in Gulf News — 22 May 2017

“Desert Cubs Cricket Academy became the first team to emerge as champions of the ECB Women’s Cricket League after remaining unbeaten throughout the two-month long competition — defeating GM Cricket Academy by 67 runs in the Final.”

— K.R. Nayer, Chief Cricket Writer, Gulf News

Desert Cubs women’s team with coaches after winning the ECB Women’s Cricket League Final, May 2017
